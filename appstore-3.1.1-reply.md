Hello, and thank you for the detailed feedback.

We have submitted a new build that removes every in-app reference to plans or
subscriptions purchased outside the app:

- The "Top Up" button (which opened an external web page) has been removed on iOS.
- The plan tier and expiration date are no longer displayed on iOS.
- Wording that referenced upgrading to a paid "Pro" plan has been replaced with
  neutral text that does not mention purchasing or direct users anywhere to buy.

We would also like to provide context on how the guidelines apply to our product,
in case it is helpful for this and future reviews:

Navitag is a companion app for **physical GPS tracker hardware** that our customers
buy as a one-time physical purchase. The recurring "plan" associated with each
tracker is the **cellular connectivity and data-hosting service that keeps the
physical device reporting its location** — a real-world service tied to a physical
good and consumed outside the app, comparable to the service activation for any
connected hardware device (Guideline 3.1.3(e)). The iOS app itself does not sell or
unlock any standalone digital content; it functions as a **viewer and controller**
for tracker hardware the customer already owns.

With the purchase surfaces now removed from iOS, the app simply displays data from
devices the customer has already activated. If, given this context, you still
require an In-App Purchase for any part of the experience, we would be grateful for
clarification on which functionality you consider in scope so we can address it
correctly.

Thank you again for your time and guidance.
